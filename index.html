3. Drug chart (manual entry)

Enter only what is prescribed/administered and verified. Do not guess missing doses or rates. The selected date shows only that day's drug entries.

Today · New daily chart
STARTIVFInfusionREGULARSOS
Drug categoryRequired details for selected categoryAction

START, REGULAR and SOS contain medication details only, with no fluid-rate calculations. IVF has a fluid selector (RL, NS, DNS, ½NS, 10D), an optional alternating RL/NS plan, prescribed rate (mL/hour), and giving-set drop factor to calculate drops/min directly from the prescribed rate. Infusion records the drug name and total mixed-fluid volume, then calculates mL/hour from the entered duration. The chart groups and colour-codes each category. SOS is administered only when prescribed indication and parameters are met.

4. Vital signs chart (manual entry)

Record actual measured values, units and observation time. Red highlighting is an adult screening flag only: HR below 60 or above 100/min; RR below 12 or above 20/min; temperature below 36°C or 38°C and above; SpO₂ below 94%; systolic BP below 90 or BP 140/90 and above. These are configurable clinical-context flags, not diagnoses. Use age- and patient-specific parameters and escalate according to hospital policy.

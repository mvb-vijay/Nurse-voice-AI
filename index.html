1. Recognition settings

Multilingual Whisper · On-device processing
Connect to Wi-Fi and load your selected model. First download may take several minutes.

Each model must be downloaded once before it can work offline. Switching to a model not yet cached requires internet.

Realme 6: Whisper Base may be slow or exceed available memory on some phone configurations. If Chrome closes or the phone becomes very slow, choose Whisper Tiny.

2. Record a short clinical dictation

Use a quiet environment. Keep the phone 15–20 cm from your mouth. Speak one short clinical statement at a time. Maximum recording: 60 seconds.

No recording yet.

3. Review and edit transcription

Clinical verification required. Check patient identifiers, diagnosis, drug name, dose, route, time, vital sign, decimal, unit and negative findings against the source. This app does not validate clinical truth.

4. Manual clinical verification checklist

Before using a note, manually verify BP, pulse, respiratory rate, SpO₂ and oxygen device, temperature and unit, GCS components, infusion drug/concentration/rate, medication dose/route/time, intake, urine output and drains.

Privacy and limitations

Audio is processed in the browser by the selected downloaded model. This prototype does not intentionally upload recordings to a transcription server. Do not enter identifiable patient information or use this public prototype for real patient records. Browser storage may be cleared. Whisper may misrecognize Indian-accented English, Telugu, mixed speech, medical terms and numbers. Never use output without human verification.

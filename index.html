Privacy and clinical safety

Audio is processed in your browser by the downloaded model; this app does not upload recordings to a transcription server. Do not use identifiable patient information in this prototype. Always verify names, drug doses, routes, times, vital signs, units and clinical findings against the source before copying anything into an official record. AI transcription can make clinically important errors.
